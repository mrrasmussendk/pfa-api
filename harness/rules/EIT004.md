# EIT004 — Undeclared slice dependency
Each slice carries a `slice.json` manifest (`{"depends_on": ["embeddings"]}`) that is its seam: you can reach exactly what you declare. EIT004 enforces it — importing `slices.<x>.contract` from a slice whose manifest does not list `x` fails the check. The manifest is also what `heimdall map` turns into the dependency graph, fan-in, and the `AGENTS.md` deps lines, so the map agents read cannot drift from the code.
**Fix:** add the slice to `depends_on` (and ask whether the seam is cut right), or stop importing it.
